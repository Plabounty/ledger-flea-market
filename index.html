New Flea Market Opens Its Doors in Lampe

Antiques, collectibles and a few things nobody can quite explain — all under one roof, with booths still available.

FROM THE FRONT COUNTER

Lampe has a new place to lose an afternoon. Ledger Flea Market is now open, filled booth by booth with vendors selling the kind of things that don't come off a shipping truck — old furniture, glassware, tools, signs, toys, records and more.

The idea is simple: every item has a history, and somebody kept track of it. That's where the name comes from. Shoppers can expect the stock to change week to week as vendors bring in new finds, so the regulars come back often.

And for the curious, there's a third category worth a slow walk: oddities. The strange, the peculiar and the conversation pieces you'll want to explain to your friends.

Vendors interested in setting up shop can rent a booth by the month, and folks with a house full of stuff can rent indoor garage sale space — no driveway, no weather, no problem.
